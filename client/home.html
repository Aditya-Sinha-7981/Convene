    What it does

    Built for the meeting, not the cloud.

    A microphone per person

    Each phone streams its own audio, so every line is attributed to the person who joined on it.

    Honest about uncertainty

    When Convene is not sure who spoke, it marks the line Needs review. Fix it in one click; the original stays in the audit record.

    Ask the meeting

    Ask a question while you talk. Answers cite who said it and when, and Convene says so when nobody did.

    Summary and minutes

    When the meeting ends you get a structured summary with decisions and action items, ready to share.

    How it works

    Three steps, no accounts.

    1. 1

      Start a meeting

      Open Convene on the laptop and create a meeting.

    2. 2

      Scan the QR code

      Everyone joins from their phone's browser on the same Wi-Fi.

    3. 3

      Just talk

      Watch the transcript fill in, ask questions, and get the summary at the end.

    Ready when your room is.

    No sign-up, no upload. Start a meeting and hand the QR code around.
